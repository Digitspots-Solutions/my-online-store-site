About Aallie Cooperative Tech Limited

Who We Are

Aallie Cooperative Tech Limited (also known as Aallie Esusu Digital Co) is a Nigerian fintech company dedicated to transforming how Nigerians save and invest through cooperative systems. Based in Ikorodu, Lagos, we leverage cutting-edge treasury technology to bring transparency, security, and convenience to traditional esusu and cooperative savings models.

Our Mission

We exist to democratize access to structured savings and investment opportunities by digitizing trusted community-based financial practices. Through our platform, we make it easier for individuals, groups, and cooperatives to manage contributions, track savings, and achieve collective financial success.

Why Choose Aallie?

  • Built by Nigerians for Nigerians - We understand local savings culture
  • Treasury-grade security - Led by experienced treasury technology professionals
  • Transparent operations - Real-time tracking of all contributions and disbursements
  • Mobile-first design - Save and manage funds from anywhere
  • Community-focused - Strengthening the cooperative tradition with modern tools

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aallie Cooperative Tech Limited?

Aallie (also known as Aallie Esusu Digital Co) is a Nigerian fintech platform that digitizes traditional cooperative savings systems like esusu. We provide secure, transparent tools for managing group savings, contributions, and disbursements.

How does digital esusu work?

Members register on our platform, agree on contribution amounts and schedules, then make regular digital payments. The system automatically tracks all contributions and manages payout rotations with complete transparency. Every transaction is recorded and accessible to authorized members.

Is my money safe with Aallie?

Absolutely. We employ treasury-grade security measures and comply with Nigerian financial regulations. Our platform is led by experienced treasury technology professionals who prioritize the security of your funds. All transactions are encrypted and monitored.

What types of groups can use Aallie?

Any savings group can benefit: traditional esusu circles, cooperative societies, thrift collectors, investment clubs, community development associations, workplace savings groups, and family savings pools.

How much does it cost?

We offer flexible pricing based on group size and features needed. Contact us for a customized quote. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Do I need special technical skills?

No. Our platform is designed to be user-friendly for everyone. If you can use WhatsApp or mobile banking, you can use Aallie. We also provide training and ongoing support.

How do members make contributions?

Members can contribute via bank transfer, debit card, or mobile money. We integrate with all major Nigerian payment platforms for maximum convenience.

Can we customize the platform for our cooperative?

Yes. We work with each group to configure contribution schedules, payout rules, and administrative controls to match your specific needs and bylaws.

What happens if a member misses a contribution?

The system sends automatic reminders before due dates. Groups can set their own policies for late payments. Our platform helps maintain accountability while allowing for flexibility when needed.

How do we get started?

Simply contact us via phone or WhatsApp at +234 803 395 6759. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your needs and provide a demonstration of the platform tailored to your cooperative.
